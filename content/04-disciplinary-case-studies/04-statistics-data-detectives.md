# Statistics Data Detectives

Statistics data detective activities turn data analysis into a mystery that students must solve. Students can examine graphs, tables, and surveys to find patterns, compare groups, and decide what the data means. Gamification gives students a role like being a data detective, and a goal such as finding the best explanation for a set of clues. 

This image shows a data detective activity with graphs, a table, a magnifying glass, and statistical clues.

![A statistic data detective dashboard showing bar graphs, scatter plots, data tables, and a magnifying glass](statistics-data-detectives.jpg)

## Looking for Evidence

Students can work with a data set and search for evidence that answers a question. They may compare the mean and median, look for an outlier, or decide whether a graph shows a trend. The activity can give students points for using evidence clearly rather than simply guessing.

## Using Digital Data Tools

A simulation or graphing tool can help students organize data and create visuals. Students can change the data, add a new point, or remove an outlier to see how the graph and summary statistics change. This helps students understand that data can tell different stories depending on how it is collected and displayed.

## Explaining the Case

At the end of the activity students can explain their conclusion using the evidence they found. They will need to describe the trend, support their answer with numbers, and explain why their conclusion makes sense.

### Students can...
- Examine their graphs and tables.
- Compare measures of center.
- Look for trends and outliers.
- Explain their conclusions with evidence.
