# Graphing Challenges

Graphing challenges combine digital graphing tools with gamification. Students can enter equations, change values, and watch the graph respond. The gamified part gives students a goal like matching a target graph, finding the correct slope, or completing a group challenge before moving to the next level. 


## Matching Graphs and Equations

A graphing challenge can show students a target line or curve and ask them to create the matching equation. Students can change the slope, y-intercept, or other values until their graph matches the target. This turns graphing into an active problem solving activity instead of only copying equations from a worksheet. 

## Learning Through Levels

Challenges can begin with simple linear equations and more towards quadratic, exponential, or absolute value functions. Students can earn a point or badge after they correctly identify how an equation changes a graph. The purpose of the points is to recognize understanding and not to make students rush.

### Students can work through a graphing challenge by:

- Looking closely at the target graph.
- Testing one change in the equation.
- Comparing the new graph to the target.
- Explaining why the graph changed.

## Checking and Explaining

A graphing tool can quickly show whether a student is close to the correct answer. However the student should still explain their thinking. A correct graph is important, but understanding the slope, intercept, and shape of the graph is what make the activity meaningful.
