# What are Math Simulations

Math simulations are interactive digital tools that allow students to explore mathematical ideas by changing numbers, moving objects, and observing what happens. Instead of only reading an equation or solving a problem ion paper students can see how graphs, shapes, patters, and data change in real time.

This image shows a digital math simulation with an equation, graph, sliders, and changing values.

![A computer screen showing an equation, graph, sliders, and changing mathematical values](what-are-math-simulations.png)

## Learning by Exploring

Math simulations give students a chance to explore before they know every answer. A student may change a number in an equation and watch the graph move. They may adjust the length of a side in a shape and notice how the area or perimeter changes. This can help students connect a math rule to something they can see. 

## Changing Variables

Many math simulations allow students to change variables. They can move sliders, type in a different number, or drag points on a graph. When one number changes students can observe how it affects the rest of the equation or model. This makes it easier to see how different parts of the equation work together.

## Making Math Visible

Some math ideas can feel difficult when students only see numbers and symbols. Simulations can make those ideas more visible by showing a graph, and table, a shape, or a model that changes with the math. Students can spend times exploring intend of only memorizing a rule.

> A math simulation gives students a way to see the results of a change instead of only imagining it.
