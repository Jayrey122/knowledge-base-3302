# Simulation Tools

Simulation tools can make high school mathematics more interactive by allowing students to change numbers, move points, and see what happens right away. Instead of only looking at equations on paper students can use digital tools to explore graphs, geometry, functions, and mathematical patterns. When simulators are combined with gamification students can work towards goals, complete challenges, unlock levels, and track their progress as they learn. 

This section looks at hoe math simulations and game like activities can work together. A student can use a graphing tool to match an equation to a target graph, complete a geometry mission by creating a shape with specific measurements, or move through function levels by explaining how an equation changes a graph. These activities can make math feel more active because students are able to test ideas, make predictions, and see the results of their choices.
