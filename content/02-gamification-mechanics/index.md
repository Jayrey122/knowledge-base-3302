Date: 2026-09-28

Gamification mechanics are game inspired features that make an activity more interactive. They can give students goals to work toward and show progression. The most common mechanics include points, badges, levels, challenges, and leaderboards.

This section looks at how these mechanics work individually and how they can work together. A student might earn points for completing a task, receive a badge after reaching an important goal, unlock a new level, and when they partner up with others to create a challenge. When these elements are planned well they can make students feel more involved and motivated to continue.
