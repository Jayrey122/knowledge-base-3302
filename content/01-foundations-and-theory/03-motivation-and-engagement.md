# Motivation and Engagement

Motivation and engagement are important because students are more likely to participate when they understand the goal of an activity and feel that their effort matters. Gamification can support motivation by giving students clear goals, useful feedback, choices, and visible progress.

This image shows a learner moving through checkpoints toward a goal representing progress and continues participation.

![A learner moving along a path with checkpoints towards a final goal](motivation-and-engagement.jpeg)

## Clear Goals

Students may feel more motivated when they know exactly what they are working toward. A goal can be a challenge to complete a skill to practice or a level to reach. Clear goals make an activity feel more organized and can help students focus on what they need to do next.

## Feedback and Progress

Feedback helps students understand what they are doing well and what they need to improve. Progress bars, points, and checklists can also make growth easier to notice. When students see that they are getting closer to a goal they may be more willing to keep working even when the task becomes difficult.

## Choice and Participation

Giving students choices can make them feel more involved. They may choose which challenge to complete first, what role to take in a group, or how to show what they learned. When students have some control they may feel more responsible for their own progress.
