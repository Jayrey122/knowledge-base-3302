# Foundations and Theory

Foundations and theory explain the main ideas behind gamification and why it can affect the way students participate in learning behind gamification and why it can affect the way students participate in learning activities. Before using points, badges, levels, or challenges it is important to understand what gamification means and how goals, feedback, choice, and motivation can influence student engagement.

This section introduces the basic concepts that connect gamification to learning. Students may feel more involved when they have a clear goal, can see their progress, receive feedback, and have opportunities to make choices. Gamification can support learning when the game like features encourage students to keep trying.
