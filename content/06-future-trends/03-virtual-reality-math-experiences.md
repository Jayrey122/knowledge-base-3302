# Virtual Reality Math Experiences

Virtual reality can give students new ways to explore mathematics by placing graphs, shapes, and models in a three dimensional digital space. Students will be able to walk around a geometric figure, view a graph from different angles, or complete a math challenge in a virtual anvironment.

This image shows a student using a virtual reality headset to explore three dimensional geometry, graphs, and mathematics models.

![A student wearing a virtual reality headset while viewing three dimensional shapes, graphs, and a coordinate planes](virtual-reality-math-experiences.jpg)

## Exploring Math in 3D

Some high school math ideas are easier to understand when students can see them from more than one view. Virtual reality can help student explore three dimensional shapes, planes, volume, and transformations. Instead of only looking at a picture on a page students could move around a model and examine it closely. 

## Adding Challenges 

Gamification can make a virtual reality activity feel more purposeful. Students can complete a geometry mission, collect evidence about a shape, or unlock a new section of a virtual space after solving a problem. The challenge should still require students to explain the math they used. 

## Thinking About Access

Virtual reality may be exciting, but it will not be available for every classroom. Schools need to think about cost, equipment, comfort, accessibility, and whether the technology helps students learn better than another tool. 

> New technology is most useful when it makes a difficult idea easier to explore not when it is used only because it looks impressive.
