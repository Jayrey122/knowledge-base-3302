# Personalized Math Pathways

Personalized math pathways allow students to move through learning activities at different speeds. Instead of every student completing the same challenge in the same order students can choose from different practice tasks, revisit a skill, or move ahead when they are ready. 

## Different Paths to Understand

Students have the same learning goal but need different steps to reach it. One student might need to practice graphing linear equations before moving forward while another might be ready to explore quadratic functions. A personalized pathway can give students more time when they need it.

## Choice and Progress

Gamification can make personalized learning paths more engaging. Students can choose which challenge to complete first, earn progress for improving, or unlock a new level after explaining their work correctly. This gives students a clearer picture of what they have learned and what they should work on next.

## Staying Connected

Personalized learning should not mean students always work alone. Students can still discuss math ideas, solve problems with partners, and share strategies. The goal is to give students useful support while keeping the classroom connected.
